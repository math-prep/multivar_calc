## 1. Problem Overview
Setting up double integrals in orders $dx dy$ and $dy dx$ over triangle and circular sector.

---

## 2. Key Formulas and Theorems

### Order Formulations
- Horizontal Slices ($dx dy$): $\int_0^2 \int_{-y/2}^0 f dx dy$
- Vertical Slices ($dy dx$): $\int_{-1}^0 \int_{-2x}^2 f dy dx$
- Non-simple regions require splitting into sum of integrals.
