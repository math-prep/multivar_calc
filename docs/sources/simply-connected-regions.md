# Simply connected regions

**Source:** MIT 18.02SC Multivariable Calculus, Fall 2010

---

## Key Formulas and Theorems

### Definition of Simply Connected Region
A region is **simply connected** if every simple closed curve in the region can be continuously contracted to a point without leaving the region.

---

### Classification of the 6 Regions

1. **$\mathbb{R}^3$:**
   - **Simply Connected:** Any closed loop can shrink to a point within 3D space.

2. **$\mathbb{R}^3 \setminus \{	ext{z-axis}\}$:**
   - **Not Simply Connected:** Loops encircling the z-axis cannot shrink to a point without intersecting the removed line.

3. **$\mathbb{R}^3 \setminus \{(0,0,0)\}$:**
   - **Simply Connected:** Removing a single point in 3D does not obstruct loops, as loops can be deformed around the point in the third dimension.

4. **$\mathbb{R}^3 \setminus \{	ext{circle}\}$:**
   - **Not Simply Connected:** Loops threading through the circle cannot be contracted without hitting the removed circle.

5. **$\mathbb{R}^2 \setminus \{	ext{line segment}\}$:**
   - **Not Simply Connected:** In 2D, loops enclosing the line segment cannot contract without intersecting it.

6. **Solid Torus (Doughnut):**
   - **Not Simply Connected:** Loops passing around the central hole of the torus cannot be shrunk within the solid torus.
