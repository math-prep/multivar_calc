## 1. Problem Overview
Evaluating $\oint_C \vec{F} \cdot d\vec{r}$ around circle $C$ of radius $b$ without parametrization.

---

## 2. Key Formulas and Theorems

### Geometric Principles
- Radial field $\vec{F} = \langle x, y \rangle \perp \hat{T} \implies \oint_C \vec{F} \cdot d\vec{r} = 0$.
- Tangential field $\vec{F} = \langle -y, x \rangle \parallel \hat{T}$, $|\vec{F}|=b \implies \oint_C \vec{F} \cdot d\vec{r} = b(2\pi b) = 2\pi b^2$.
