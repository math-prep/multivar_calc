## 1. Problem Overview
1. Angle $\theta$ between $\vec{v} = \hat{i}+\hat{j}+2\hat{k}$ and $\vec{w} = 2\hat{i}-\hat{j}+\hat{k}$.
2. Parameter $a$ for right angle $\angle PQR$ with $P(a,1,-1), Q(0,1,1), R(a,-1,3)$.

---

## 2. Key Formulas and Theorems

### Angle Formula
$$\cos\theta = \frac{\vec{v} \cdot \vec{w}}{|\vec{v}| |\vec{w}|} = \frac{1(2) + 1(-1) + 2(1)}{\sqrt{6}\sqrt{6}} = \frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2} \implies \theta = \frac{\pi}{3}$$

### Orthogonality Condition
$$\vec{QP} \cdot \vec{QR} = 0 \implies \langle a, 0, -2 \rangle \cdot \langle a, -2, 2 \rangle = 0 \implies a^2 - 4 = 0 \implies a = \pm 2$$
