## 2. Key Formulas and Theorems

### Contour Topology Rules
- Concentric loops enclose a local minimum or local maximum.
- Pass between two adjacent peaks/valleys forms a **saddle point** ($
nabla f = \vec{0}$, Hessian determinant $< 0$).
