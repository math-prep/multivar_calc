## 1. Problem Overview
Mass, centroid, and moments of inertia for unit square $[0,1]^2$ with density $\delta(x,y) = xy$.

---

## 2. Key Formulas and Theorems

### Formulas
$$M = \iint_R xy dA = \frac{1}{4}$$
$$\bar{x} = \frac{1}{M} \iint_R x(xy) dA = 4\left(\frac{1}{6}\right) = \frac{2}{3}, \quad \bar{y} = \frac{2}{3}$$
$$I_0 = \iint_R (x^2+y^2)xy dA = \frac{1}{4}, \quad I_x = \iint_R y^2(xy) dA = \frac{1}{8}$$
