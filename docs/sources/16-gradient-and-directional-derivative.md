## 1. Problem Overview
Computing gradients and directional derivatives $D_{\hat{u}}f = \nabla f \cdot \hat{u}$.

---

## 2. Key Formulas and Theorems

### Formulas
$$\nabla f = \left\langle \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_1}, \dots, \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_n} \right\rangle, \quad D_{\hat{u}} f = \nabla f \cdot \frac{\vec{v}}{|\vec{v}|}$$
