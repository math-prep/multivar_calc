# Green's Theorem in Normal Form

**Video URL**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1ye7dm9aUj0  
**Course**: MIT 18.02SC Multivariable Calculus, Fall 2010

---

## 1. Problem Overview
Verifying $\oint_C \vec{F} \cdot \hat{n} ds = \iint_R \text{div}(\vec{F}) dA$ for $\vec{F} = \langle x, y \rangle$.

---

## 2. Key Formulas and Theorems

### Normal Form
$$\text{div}(\vec{F}) = 1 + 1 = 2 \implies \iint_R 2 dA = 2 \cdot \text{Area} = \pi$$
