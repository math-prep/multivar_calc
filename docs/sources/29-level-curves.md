## 1. Problem Overview
Graphing level curves $f(x,y) = c$.

---

## 2. Key Formulas and Theorems

### Types of Level Curves
- $2x + y = c$: Parallel lines $y = -2x + c$.
- $x^2 + y^2 = c$: Concentric circles of radius $\sqrt{c}$.
- $x^2 - y^2 = c$: Hyperbolas with asymptotes $y = \pm x$.
