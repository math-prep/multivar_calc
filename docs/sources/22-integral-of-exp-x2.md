## 1. Problem Overview
Gaussian integral $I = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2} dx$.

---

## 2. Key Formulas and Theorems

### Polar Coordinate Substitution
$$I^2 = \iint_{\mathbb{R}^2} e^{-(x^2+y^2)} dx dy = \int_0^{2\pi} d\theta \int_0^{\infty} e^{-r^2} r dr = (2\pi)\left(\frac{1}{2}\right) = \pi \implies I = \sqrt{\pi}$$
