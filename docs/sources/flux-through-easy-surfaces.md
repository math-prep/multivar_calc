## Key Formulas and Theorems

### Part (a): Flux of $ec{F} = \hat{k}$ through Infinite Cylinder $x^2 + y^2 = 1$
- **Normal Vector Orientation:**
  The normal $\hat{n}$ to the cylinder $x^2 + y^2 = 1$ is parallel to the xy-plane and has no z-component ($\hat{n} \cdot \hat{k} = 0$).
- **Flux Calculation:**
  $$	ext{Flux} = \iint_S ec{F} \cdot \hat{n} \, dS = \iint_S (\hat{k} \cdot \hat{n}) \, dS = \iint_S 0 \, dS = 0$$
