## 1. Problem Overview
Equations of planes from normal vectors, points, parallel vectors, and three points.

---

## 2. Key Formulas and Theorems

### Point-Normal Form
$$a(x-x_0) + b(y-y_0) + c(z-z_0) = 0$$

### Cross Product Normal
$$\vec{N} = \vec{v}_1 \times \vec{v}_2 \quad \text{or} \quad \vec{N} = (Q - P) \times (R - P)$$

### Parallel Planes Formula
Parallel planes share normal $\vec{N}=\langle 1,2,3 \rangle \implies x + 2y + 3z - 14 = 0$ for point $(1,2,3)$.
