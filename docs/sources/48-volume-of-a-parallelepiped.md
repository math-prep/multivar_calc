## 1. Problem Overview
Calculating volume of parallelepiped spanned by vectors from origin.

---

## 2. Key Formulas and Theorems

### Scalar Triple Product / Determinant Formula
$$\text{Volume} = |\det(A)| = \left| \begin{vmatrix} u_x & u_y & u_z \\ v_x & v_y & v_z \\ w_x & w_y & w_z \end{vmatrix} \right|$$
If $\det(A) = -4 \implies \text{Volume} = 4$.
