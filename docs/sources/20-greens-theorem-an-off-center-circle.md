## 1. Problem Overview
Evaluating line integral over off-center circle centered at $(a,0)$ with radius $a$.

---

## 2. Key Formulas and Theorems

### Reduction & Centroid
$$\oint_C M dx + N dy = \iint_R 4x dA = 4 \bar{x} \text{Area}(R) = 4(a)(\pi a^2) = 4\pi a^3$$
