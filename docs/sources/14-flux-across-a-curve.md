## 1. Problem Overview
Geometric flux calculations $\int_C \vec{F} \cdot \hat{n} ds$ across circle and line segment.

---

## 2. Key Formulas and Theorems

### Radial Field Flux
$$\vec{F} \parallel \hat{n} \implies \text{Flux} = g(1) (2\pi) = 2\pi g(1)$$

### Tangential Field Flux
$$\vec{F} \perp \hat{n} \implies \text{Flux} = 0$$

### Uniform Field Flux
$$\text{Flux} = (\vec{F} \cdot \hat{n}) \cdot \text{Length} = (-3\sqrt{2})(\sqrt{2}) = -6$$
