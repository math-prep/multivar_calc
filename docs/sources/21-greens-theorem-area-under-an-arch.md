# Green's Theorem: Area Under an Arch

**Video URL**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KXof0q88xbg  
**Course**: MIT 18.02SC Multivariable Calculus, Fall 2010

---

## 1. Problem Overview
Area enclosed by cycloid arch $x = a(\theta - \sin\theta), y = a(1 - \cos\theta)$ and x-axis.

---

## 2. Key Formulas and Theorems

### Area via Line Integral
$$\text{Area} = -\oint_C y dx = \int_0^{2\pi} a(1 - \cos\theta) \cdot a(1 - \cos\theta) d\theta = 3\pi a^2$$
