## 1. Problem Overview
Finding intersection of two 2D parametric lines $L_1(t)$ and $L_2(u)$.

---

## 2. Key Formulas and Theorems

### Dummy Variables Distinction
Use independent parameters $t$ for $L_1$ and $u$ for $L_2$:
$$L_1: \begin{cases} x = 2 - t \\ y = 1 + t \end{cases}, \quad L_2: \begin{cases} x = 2 + u \\ y = 4 + 2u \end{cases}$$

### System Equations
$$2 - t = 2 + u \implies t = -u$$
$$1 + t = 4 + 2u \implies 1 - u = 4 + 2u \implies 3u = -3 \implies u = -1, t = 1$$

### Intersection Point
$$(x,y) = (1, 2)$$
