## 1. Problem Overview
Given $\vec{r}(t)$ with $|\vec{r}| = c$ and $\vec{a}(t) = k \vec{r}(t)$, proving orthogonality $\vec{r} \cdot \vec{v} = 0$ and constancy of $\vec{r} \times \vec{v}$.

---

## 2. Key Formulas and Theorems

### Dot Product Derivative
$$\vec{r} \cdot \vec{r} = c^2 \implies \frac{d}{dt}(\vec{r} \cdot \vec{r}) = 2 \vec{r} \cdot \vec{v} = 0 \implies \vec{r} \cdot \vec{v} = 0$$

### Cross Product Derivative
$$\frac{d}{dt}(\vec{r} \times \vec{v}) = \vec{v} \times \vec{v} + \vec{r} \times \vec{a} = \vec{0} + \vec{r} \times (k\vec{r}) = \vec{0} \implies \vec{r} \times \vec{v} = \text{const}$$
