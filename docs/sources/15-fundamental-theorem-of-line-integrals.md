## 1. Problem Overview
Evaluating $\int_C \nabla f \cdot d\vec{r}$ for $f(x,y) = x^5 + 3xy^3$ from $(1,0)$ to $(-1,0)$.

---

## 2. Key Formulas and Theorems

### Fundamental Theorem of Line Integrals
$$\int_C \nabla f \cdot d\vec{r} = f(P_{\text{end}}) - f(P_{\text{start}})$$
$$f(-1,0) - f(1,0) = (-1)^5 - 1^5 = -2$$
